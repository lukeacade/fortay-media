The Brief

A team burnt
by their last
video shop.

Places Gym came to us looking for two things: an informative onboarding film to send to new members, and short social video snippets they could deploy tactically around campaigns and offers.

The bigger problem was confidence. A previous video production experience - full of high costs and slow turnarounds - had left the team wary of the whole process. They needed reassurance, then they needed work that backed it up.

After meeting in our Sheffield studio, the team relaxed, and we got going. From that point on, the engagement ran on 1–2 day amends turnarounds and a workflow that kept them involved at every step.

"I can definitely see Places Gym using video again in the future!"

- Natalie Young · Marketing Manager, Places Gym

Scope of
work.

A focused video engagement, designed to give the marketing team assets that worked across onboarding, organic and paid - all from a single shoot day.

01 - Onboarding

New Member Welcome Film

An informative, welcoming film built to give new members confidence ahead of their first session - leaning into Places' community feel, inclusive classes and value pricing against the bigger chains.

02 - Production

On-Site Filming

We travelled to the gym to shoot the welcome film and the social snippets in one trip - capturing real members, real spaces, and the inclusive atmosphere that defines the Places brand.

03 - Social Cuts

Snippets for Campaigns

Short-form social videos tactically aligned to the Places campaign calendar - designed to be dropped in around offers and announcements, both organic and paid.

04 - Workflow

1–2 Day Amends

Vimeo review links, fast revisions, and a clear handover - turning what had been a slow process for the team into a workflow they could plan campaigns around.
