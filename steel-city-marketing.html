The Brief

The people
behind the pen.

Steel City Marketing is the Sheffield business behind the branded merchandise you don't always notice - the uniform, the pen, the promotional kit that makes another company look good. After a soft rebrand, they wanted to turn that attention on themselves, reintroducing their own brand to existing clients while sharing the marketing expertise they'd built up over years in the industry.

Working closely with Steel City's Marketing Executive, our video team set out to put the people and the place front and centre - showing the professional services on offer through the personalities actually delivering them.

We started with a single video, #AskTheExpert, featuring Managing Director James inviting the audience to send in their branded-merchandise questions. The response shaped everything that followed.

"We knew we could leave FORTAYmedia to do what they do, and produce something that just looks amazing."

Scope of
work.

A running social content partnership - built around a real team, real questions and a series that grew from audience feedback. Below is exactly what we delivered.

01 - Discovery

#AskTheExpert Pilot

A single video with Managing Director James inviting audience questions on branded merchandise - testing the format and gauging what the audience actually wanted to know.

02 - Strategy

Brand Out, Stand Out Series

Built directly from #AskTheExpert feedback, a recurring social series answering the most popular questions alongside tips and statistics that showcase the business's expertise.

03 - Production

Team-Led Filming

The Steel City team starring as themselves - planned, directed and shot to capture a dynamic that's both fun and genuinely professional, on location in Sheffield.

04 - Delivery

Edit & Social Formatting

Edit, sound and captioning tailored for social feeds, giving the brand a personal touch that carried through every video in the series.
