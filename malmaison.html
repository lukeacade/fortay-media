The Brief

A boutique stay,
sold on feel.

Housed in the former offices of Leeds' tramways HQ, Malmaison sits in the pulsating heart of the city - a stone's throw from designer shops and the best of the nightlife. Looking to grow both business and leisure bookings, the hotel came to Fortay for a digital marketing overhaul that could do justice to the building's character.

Brochure shots weren't going to cut it. We proposed a Hotel Video Tour told through the eyes of a guest - following her from check-in through to the moments that make a stay memorable, so viewers get a first-hand feel for the hotel and its facilities rather than a list of amenities.

Video was only the start. Our social team took on the day-to-day of keeping over 5,000 Twitter followers and Facebook fans up to date, alongside a monthly newsletter covering the best of the hotel and the city around it.

"FORTAYmedia really understood what we wanted and what we were about, and had good knowledge of hospitality."

Scope of
work.

A hospitality brief that ran from concept and shoot through to the ongoing rhythm of social and email. Here's what we built for Malmaison Leeds.

01 - Strategy

Guest-Eye Concept

We built the tour around a single guest's journey - arrival, room, facilities, night out - so the film sold an experience, not a spec sheet.

02 - Production

Hotel Video Tour

On-location filming across the hotel's rooms, bar and public spaces, directed to capture the boutique feel that sets Malmaison apart from a standard city hotel.

03 - Social

Community Management

Day-to-day management of the hotel's Twitter and Facebook presence - keeping over 5,000 followers and fans engaged with what's happening at the hotel and in the city.

04 - Ongoing

Monthly Newsletter

A recurring newsletter pulling together hotel news and city highlights, keeping past guests warm and future ones curious.
