Keeping your site current is what keeps customers engaged and search engines interested - it also tells anyone landing on your homepage that you're an active, trustworthy business. Our bespoke CMS is built around that single job.

01 - Capability

Edit Text Freely

Update headlines, pricing, service descriptions, and copy across any page the moment something changes in your business - no ticket, no waiting on a developer.

  • Inline text editing on every page
  • No code or markup exposed to you
  • Changes go live the instant you publish
02 - Capability

Swap Images & Media

Refresh hero banners, product shots, and galleries yourself as your offering evolves, your season changes, or your stock photography simply gets old.

  • Drag-and-drop image replacement
  • Automatic resizing and optimisation
  • Full control over galleries and banners
03 - Capability

Add New Pages

Launch a landing page for a campaign, a new service, or a seasonal offer without commissioning a new build every time you have a new idea.

  • Build new pages from existing templates
  • Structured, on-brand layouts by default
  • No coding or design skills required
04 - Capability

Sales & Offers

Run promotions, update pricing tables, and manage time-limited offers directly - built specifically for the pace of B2B, B2C, and e-commerce businesses.

  • Dedicated offers and promotions management
  • Scheduled publishing for campaign dates
  • Works across B2B, B2C and e-commerce sites
05 - Capability

Approval Workflows

Give your team the ability to draft and submit pages, while an administrator reviews and approves before anything goes public. Full control, zero surprises.

  • Draft, submit, and approve stages
  • Administrator sign-off before publishing
  • Full visibility over who changed what
06 - Capability

User Permissions

Decide exactly who on your team can access which parts of the system - from a single content editor to a full marketing department with defined roles.

  • Granular, role-based access control
  • Safe for teams of any size
  • Only basic training required to get started
