The Brief

A local operator
with national
ambition.

DJB Recycling is the business behind your business's waste - offering a total waste management solution for organisations across the UK. A capable team, a good service, and a digital footprint that wasn't pulling its weight.

We've been working together since 2013, with one consistent goal: build DJB's online marketing presence to the point that demand outruns the day-to-day. Over the years, that has helped take sales from 50 bins a week to 115 bins a day.

The work has always sat at the intersection of web, video and social - three legs of a strategy that compound on each other, with one team handling all of it under one roof.

"I did have a picture in mind - and FORTAYmedia have achieved better than that."

- Darren Bland · Managing Director, DJB Recycling

Scope of
work.

From a tidy-up of three legacy websites to an ongoing content engine - every layer built to keep DJB's digital marketing presence growing.

01 - Web

One Site, Done Properly

After DJB's 2015 expansion, we consolidated three separate websites into a single, easy-to-use platform with up-to-date features, a clean layout and simplified user journeys. One source of truth, ready to scale.

02 - Visibility

SEO & Social

With the new site live, we layered on SEO and social media services - introducing DJB to a completely new audience and turning organic search into a reliable channel for enquiries.

03 - Video

In-House Video Production

A library of videos for on- and off-line use - social cuts, case study films, plus a series of explainer videos the DJB team can use to walk customers through their machines and the benefits of using them.

04 - Content

Always-On Marketing

Ongoing blogs, newsletters and videos that keep DJB visible and credible - a content cadence that has helped move the brand from a local operator to a leader in its category.
