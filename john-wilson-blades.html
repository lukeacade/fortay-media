The Brief

50 years of heritage,
one story to tell.

For half a century, National, World and Olympic champions have skated to victory on John Wilson blades. The brand's biggest challenge wasn't product quality - it was storytelling.

Before a single frame was shot, we went deep into the brand itself: its history, its products, its ethos, its audience and its most loyal followers. Understanding those 50 years gave us the tools to build a film that would resonate authentically, rather than just look good.

The result was a video that kept the blades themselves at the heart of the story, while giving the brand a human, behind-the-scenes voice it had never had on screen before.

"For views on the video across Facebook, Instagram and Twitter we're creeping up on a quarter of a million. Across the platforms we only have 25,000 followers, so we were pleasantly surprised to look at the analytics." - Tom Cantwell, Chief Executive Officer

Scope of
work.

A brand discovery process paired with lean, behind-the-scenes production - built to move fast and land hard across social. Here's exactly what we delivered.

01 - Discovery

Brand Immersion

Fifty years of history, product craft, brand ethos and audience behaviour, unpacked before a single call sheet existed - so the story would ring true to lifelong followers.

02 - Production

Behind-the-Scenes Filming

An intimate, fly-on-the-wall shoot capturing the craft behind the blades and the people who make them - direction and camera work built to feel honest, not staged.

03 - Post

Edit & Story Assembly

A tight narrative edit that balanced heritage storytelling with close, tactile shots of the product - paced for attention spans on fast-moving social feeds.

04 - Delivery

Social-First Mastering

Formats optimised for Facebook, Instagram and Twitter, ready to publish and track from launch day - built to convert a modest follower base into outsized reach.
