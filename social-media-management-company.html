Why it's worth
doing properly.

Social media is one of the few channels where a business can grow its audience and serve existing customers at the same time. Done well, it keeps you relevant. Done badly, it's forgotten - fast.

01

Stay relevant

Being active on social keeps you visible to existing customers while introducing you to new ones - in a space where brands are easily forgotten.

02

Build trust

A positive brand experience on social doesn't just retain a customer - it turns them into an advocate who recommends you to others.

03

Convert directly

Social is now a direct line to customers - for questions, complaints, and purchases - and it rewards brands who show up consistently.
