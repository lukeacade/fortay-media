The Process

Our creative team takes your brief and your brand and develops a custom animated video designed to connect with your audience and deliver results - no surprises between script and screen.

Script & Design

Every animated video starts with the story. We write the script, define the tone, and design a visual style built around your brand.

Scene by scene, we map out exactly how the video will look and flow before a single frame is animated - so there are no surprises later.
