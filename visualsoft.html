The Brief

People first,
platform second.

Visualsoft is a dedicated e-commerce provider that's grown from a single Stockton office in 1998 into a business with over 150 staff across Newcastle, Manchester and London. But the brief wasn't about the platform - it was about the people behind it.

Visualsoft approached Fortay Media to create a film that showcased the personality and ethos of the company - a piece that could attract and retain a highly-skilled workforce while promoting the reputation of the business as an employer.

The result was "It's a People Thing" - a film built around honest, on-camera conversations with the team, filmed in the environment they work in every day.

"Thank you Fortay Media, it's just how I imagined it." - Emma Hart, HR Manager, Visualsoft

Scope of
work.

A people-led production - planned, filmed and edited around real staff and a real working office. Below is exactly what we delivered.

01 - Strategy

Creative Development

Working with HR to define the story, the tone and the questions that would draw out honest answers from staff - without it ever feeling scripted or corporate.

02 - Production

On-Site Filming

Our team travelled to Visualsoft's Stockton office to interview staff on camera and capture them at work - asking what it's really like to be part of the team.

03 - Post

Edit & Sound Design

Interviews and workplace footage cut together into a single narrative, paced and scored to feel warm, human and true to the culture on the ground.

04 - Delivery

Social Cutdowns

A suite of short-form edits produced specifically for Visualsoft's social channels, extending the reach of the hero film into recruitment and employer-brand content.
