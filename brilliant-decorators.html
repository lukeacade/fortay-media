The Brief

A local trade,
rebuilt for growth.

Since 2010, Paul Pittock's Brilliant Decorators have delivered a brilliant service to residential and commercial clients alike. The problem was that the business the reputation didn't match what was happening online - Paul's existing website wasn't positioning the business, or winning it any new work.

We started with the fundamentals: a full website rebuild onto a stronger platform, followed by a dedicated SEO campaign built to generate qualified leads rather than vanity traffic. From there, the brief grew into an ongoing partnership - content, social media and video, all working together to build the brand Paul had earned but hadn't yet been given online.

The result speaks for itself: Brilliant Decorators now rank among the first results for decorators in their area, with a growing share of new work generated directly through digital marketing.

"We now have more work than we have ever had."

Scope of
work.

A full-service digital partnership - website, search and social all pulling in the same direction. Below is exactly what we delivered, from the first rebuild through to an ongoing content engine.

01 - Platform

Website Design & Development

A full rebuild onto a platform designed to convert - faster, clearer and built to actually support search visibility rather than fight against it.

02 - Search

Local SEO Strategy

On-page optimisation, technical fixes and ongoing content work aimed squarely at ranking for decorators searches in their local area - and staying there.

03 - Social

Organic & Paid Social Media

An always-on social presence combining organic community-building with targeted paid activity, introducing the business to a new, wider audience.

04 - Video

Social Video Production

A run of videos starring Paul himself - explainers and FAQs designed to be informative, memorable and engaging, and to build trust before a single quote is sent.
