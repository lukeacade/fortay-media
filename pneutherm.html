The Brief

A niche manufacturer,
ready to be seen.

Since the 1970s, PneuTherm has grown from a producer of pneumatic connectors into a genuine "one-source shop" - offering bespoke heating elements, pneumatic connectors, subcontract machining, fabrication and electrical assembly under one roof. What they didn't have was a way to tell that story.

We've partnered with PneuTherm since around 2014, when we were commissioned to design and develop their first proper website. Working closely with their marketing manager, we built a site that showcased their full product range and partner network with the clarity a technical buyer expects.

From there, the relationship grew. Our in-house production team filmed three videos on-location at their factory - including a company overview film introducing PneuTherm to new customers and partners. With no social media presence at all, we then set up, managed and distributed their content from scratch, backed by targeted campaigns, blogs and e-shots to their mailing list.

"Without a marketing partner, I think the business would have stayed fairly stagnated potentially. Having the marketing partners enabled us to continue growing even through difficult times and difficult business challenges."

Scope of
work.

A long-term, multi-discipline engagement - from the first website build through to an ongoing content and distribution partnership that continues today.

01 - Web

Website Design & Development

A brand-new site built to showcase PneuTherm's full range of bespoke and standard products, subcontract services and industry partnerships - designed around real technical buyers.

02 - Production

On-Location Factory Filming

Three videos shot at PneuTherm's factory, including a company overview film introducing the business, its people and its manufacturing capability to new customers.

03 - Social

Social Media, Built From Zero

PneuTherm had no social presence at all. We set up their channels, managed ongoing content and ran targeted campaigns to reach the right customers with the right message.

04 - Ongoing

Content & Distribution Partnership

Regular social posts, blogs and e-shots to their mailing list, with content kept optimised and up to date as part of a continuing partnership now over a decade strong.
