The video production process.

A straightforward, four-stage process that takes your video from first conversation to finished, downloadable film.

01

Firstly, we get to know you and your business. We'll discuss video elements including your message and content, and your objectives.

02

Once we have the relevant information, we begin drafting initial ideas on what we think your video should look like - actors, filming location, and filming topics.

03

Action! Filming begins - this may include any and all filming, and we'll guide you through the whole process, including making sure interviews run smoothly.

04

Once filming is complete, it's time to edit and put together your final piece. We send you the video for approval, and once you're happy it's yours to download and share.
