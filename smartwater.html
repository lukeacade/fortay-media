The Brief

A niche product,
told to the right
audience.

SmartWater Group are an award-winning risk management company, using proprietary technology to deliver material cost savings for clients in infrastructure, construction, vacant property and more - across a range of security products and services.

SmartTag, the focus of this campaign, is a product offered to the security industry to prevent, deter and detect incidents of abuse that security staff are often subject to. The challenge is one of category: most people don't actively go looking for security solutions like this - so you have to find them.

SmartWater needed a partner willing to take the time to understand the niche, the buyer, and the specific pain points - and turn that understanding into video that would resonate, not just inform.

"I'm very happy with - a. the quality, b. the turnaround, and c. the flexibility."

- Richard Tracz · Group Head of Marketing, SmartWater

Scope of
work.

An engagement that started as a product explainer and naturally expanded into long-form E-learning - a relationship built on understanding the audience first, the camera second.

01 - Understanding

Audience & Category Deep-Dive

We took the time to understand SmartWater's audience and the very specific problem SmartTag solves - so the creative decisions were driven by the buyer's mindset, not the product spec sheet.

02 - Product Film

Explainer-Led Video

A clear, well-paced explainer of SmartTag and its benefits to the security industry. We combined location filming with stock visuals to produce material that would resonate with the right audience.

03 - Distribution

Product Video Meets Social

Drawing on years of social media marketing experience, we built the video to do double duty: a product explainer that could also function as a top-of-funnel brand awareness asset.

04 - Expansion

E-Learning Content

After the initial projects, SmartWater commissioned us to put together their E-learning content - extending the relationship from a single film into a long-running content partnership.
