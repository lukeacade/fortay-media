The Process

From brief
to final
deliverable.

Four stages, one accountable team. No hand-offs, no lost details, no surprises on shoot day - just a straight line from brief to broadcast-ready film.

Start a Project
01

Concept

We work with you to develop the creative direction, script, and shot list - and agree exactly how the finished piece will be used before anyone picks up a camera.

02

Shoot

Studio or on-location, we bring the crew, the kit, and the creative eye. Tight schedules, calm sets, and producers who actually run the day.

03

Edit

Post-production handled in-house - grade, sound design, motion graphics, and titles all finished to a broadcast standard by the same team that shot it.

04

Deliver

Final files in every format you need - 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4K masters, social cut-downs - optimised for every platform you're publishing on.
