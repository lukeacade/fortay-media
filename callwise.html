Callwise
gets heard.

A new website, a library of knowledge videos and a three-month launch campaign - built to earn trust for a VoIP start-up going head-to-head with established names.

Client
Callwise
Discipline
Web Design · Video
Delivery
Web · YouTube · Social
Role
Full Production
The Brief

A start-up brand,
built on trust.

Callwise arrived with a genuinely different offer - unique VoIP systems for businesses, with no tiers, no contracts, and every feature included as standard. The challenge was making that message land fast, against competitors who'd already spent years building brand recognition.

As a start-up, Callwise needed a website that could be populated quickly and would spread knowledge of the company to the right people - and it needed to establish credibility from day one, not build it slowly over years.

With the project handled entirely out of our in-house Sheffield studio, our web, video and social teams worked side by side - designing the site, producing the explainer content, and running the campaign that introduced Callwise to the market.

"A completely unique system deserves a website that reflects that individualism - and is easy to navigate."

Scope of
work.

A full-service engagement - design, video and social under one roof. Below is exactly what we delivered, from first wireframe to launch-day post.

01 - Design

Website Design & Build

A site built around Callwise's brand guidelines and its no-tiers, no-contracts USP - populated quickly, easy to navigate, and built to convert visitors into enquiries.

02 - Trust

Testimonials & Social Proof

A dedicated testimonials strategy showcasing happy clients already using the system, giving a start-up brand the credibility signals customers look for before they buy.

03 - Video

Knowledge & Resource Films

A full suite of individual videos - from "What VoIP Is" and "How VoIP Works" to hands-on resource content like "How to Use Callwise" - filmed and edited in-house.

04 - Launch

3-Month Social Campaign

A targeted launch campaign across YouTube and Facebook, putting the video library in front of the right audience to build awareness from a standing start.
