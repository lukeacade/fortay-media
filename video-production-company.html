Common Questions

Before you get in touch.

What does a video production company actually do?

We take a project from idea to finished film: strategy and scripting, filming with the right crew and kit, and every stage of editing - grading, sound, graphics, captions - needed to get it ready for wherever it's going to live.

Do I need to already have a script or storyboard?

No. Some clients arrive with a full storyboard, others just have a business problem they know video can solve. Either way, our team will shape the concept and script with you before anything is filmed.

Can you handle both a big corporate film and ongoing social content?

Yes - that's the point of working with one production company rather than a patchwork of freelancers. The same team can deliver a flagship brand film and then keep a steady stream of social cut-downs coming afterwards.

What formats and platforms do you deliver for?

Whatever you need - 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4K masters, or platform-specific exports for YouTube, Vimeo, Meta, LinkedIn, and TikTok. We can also host and manage delivery directly to your chosen platform.

How quickly can a project turn around?

It depends on scope, but same-day highlight turnarounds for events and fast-track social edits are both things we do regularly alongside longer-lead corporate and brand productions.
