"It won't happen
to me" isn't a
security policy.

Business owners often assume their site is too small to be worth hacking. It isn't. Smaller, less-defended websites are exactly what attackers target - not always to steal from you directly, but to use your server, your visitors, and your search ranking for something else entirely.

With over 1.5 billion websites online and search engines as the front door to almost all of them, a single blacklisting event can undo years of SEO and reputation-building in a matter of hours. Only 38% of businesses say they're actually prepared to handle it.

  • Exploiting your site visitors
  • Stealing data stored on your server
  • Abusing server resources for their own use
  • Black-hat SEO and bot abuse
  • Straightforward vandalism
  • Search engine blacklisting

Multiple
layers.
One shield.

No single tool stops every attack. We combine firewalls, monitoring, and rapid response into one managed service - designed to let your business operate without fear of compromise.

01 - Service

Web Application Firewall

A WAF sits in front of your site, blocking malicious code and hacking attempts before they ever reach your server.

  • Blocks known attack signatures
  • Filters malicious traffic in real time
  • Protects site code and web apps
02 - Service

Intrusion Prevention

Malicious network activity is identified and blocked before it can be used to compromise your infrastructure.

  • Real-time network monitoring
  • Automated blocking of threats
  • Reduced exposure to exploits
03 - Service

Vulnerability Visibility

Enhanced visibility into your website surfaces the weak links where compromise or attack could occur - before someone finds them first.

  • Ongoing weak-point detection
  • Application and API scanning
  • Prioritised remediation guidance
04 - Service

Blacklist Monitoring

Your brand reputation is watched around the clock across third-party blacklists, so you're never the last to know you've been flagged.

  • Continuous blacklist checks
  • Search engine reputation protection
  • Early warning before traffic drops
05 - Service

Hack Clean-Up

If your site's already been hacked, we respond fast - removing malicious code and restoring your website to its original state.

  • Rapid malware removal
  • Full site restoration
  • Same-team recovery, no hand-offs
06 - Service

Incident Reporting

Every clean-up comes with a detailed report - what happened, why it happened, and the measures now in place to stop it happening again.

  • Root-cause breakdown
  • Security measures documented
  • Ongoing alerts for future attempts
