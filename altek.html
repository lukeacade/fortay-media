The Brief

Safety, told
through people.

ALTEK Group manufactures market-leading equipment that helps aluminium producers and recyclers extract value from waste streams, cut waste generation and boost operating productivity. For a business built on heavy machinery and process, the priority was clear: put safety front and centre, in a way people would actually engage with.

Altek had a strong idea of the tone they wanted, and brought our in-house team in to lead production. We worked closely with them from the outset - sharing early aesthetic references so they could see and approve the direction before a single frame was shot, giving them full confidence in how the film would look and feel.

The team's own families and children were part of the film, so the process had to be as relaxed and straightforward as the finished piece needed to feel - easy to watch, genuinely fun, and true to Altek's culture, while still landing the safety message with real weight.

"Between us, we worked as a team. We got a rough idea of what we wanted to do, but we were very much guided by Fortay Media." - Keith Maris, Operations Director

Scope of
work.

A close, collaborative production built around Altek's people - from early creative alignment through to a finished film ready for internal rollout.

01 - Strategy

Creative Alignment

Early conversations to understand Altek's safety priorities and culture, followed by a look-and-feel reference video so the team knew exactly what to expect before filming began.

02 - Production

On-Site Filming

A relaxed, well-managed shoot involving Altek staff and their families, filmed on location to keep the process comfortable for everyone in front of the camera.

03 - Post

Edit & Sound

A picture edit and sound pass built for warmth and clarity - keeping the film easy to watch and engaging while making sure the safety message never gets lost.

04 - Delivery

Internal Rollout

A finished film formatted for internal briefings, induction sessions and site communications, ready to be shared across the Altek team.
