The Brief

A national brand,
told with feeling.

Yorkshire Tea is more than a household name - it's a piece of British daily life. The brief was deceptively simple: produce content that captured that warmth, heritage and craft on screen, without leaning on cliché.

We were brought in to handle the project end-to-end - from creative development and production through to grading and final delivery - with a brand whose tone, voice and cultural weight had to be honoured at every step.

The film needed to flex across broadcast, social and digital, meaning every frame had to earn its place at TV quality while still cutting through on a 6-second social edit. One shoot. Multiple lives.

"Heritage isn't a backdrop. It's the reason every frame has to feel right."

Scope of
work.

A full-service engagement - creative, production and post under one roof. Below is exactly what we delivered, from the first call sheet through to the final master.

01 - Strategy

Creative Development

Concepting, treatment writing, visual references and stakeholder alignment. We translated brand strategy into a shootable creative direction the team could rally behind.

02 - Production

Full Shoot Management

Location scouting, casting, crew, lighting, sound and direction. A cinema-grade shoot built around the brand's voice - calm on set, sharp on camera.

03 - Post

Edit, Sound & Grade

Picture edit, sound design, music supervision and a full colour grade tuned for broadcast deliverables. Every cut signed off against brand and broadcast standards.

04 - Delivery

Multi-Platform Mastering

Format optimisation for TV, paid social, organic social and on-site web. One shoot reshaped into a deployable suite - without compromising the hero film.
